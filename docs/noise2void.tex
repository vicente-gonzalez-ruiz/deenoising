Another category of SSDL is based on masking. Among these, the Noise2Void approach first introduces the concept of blind spot network (BSN), which masks specific regions, such as central pixels, within images and inferring the signals of the masked regions with two simple statistical assumptions that the signal is not pixel-wise independent, and the noise is conditionally pixel-wise independent given the signal [27]. Though the assumptions of BSN are much looser than that of Noise2Noise and Noiser2Noise, there are still many scenarios which do not meet the criteria (including LDCT imaging, since the noises in LDCT image are spatially correlated, as mentioned above), thus compromising the corresponding denoising performances. % https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11077678
