  $\{i\}$ & The set $i$ \\

  $\{\bm{x}^{(\cdot)}\}$ & The set of all the instances of $\bm{x}$ \\

  $\mathsf{\href{https://docs.python.org/3/library/functions.html\#func-range}{range}}(x)$ & $=\{0, 1, \cdots, x-1\}$ \\
  $\mathsf{\href{https://numpy.org/doc/stable/reference/generated/numpy.zeros_like.html}{zeros\_like}}(\bm{x})$ & $=\{0\}_{i=0}^{\bm{x}.\mathsf{size}-1}$ \\
  % $|\mathbf{X}_i|$ & The absolute value of $\mathbf{X}_i$ \\
  $\alpha\bm{x}$ & $=\{\alpha\bm{x}_i\}$ (scalar multiplication) \\
  $\bm{x}+\bm{y}$ & $=\{\bm{x}_i + \bm{y}_i\}$ (Hadamard addition) \\ 
  $\bm{x}\bm{y}$ & $=\{\bm{x}_i\bm{y}_i\}$ (Hadamard product) \\
  $X$ & The discrete random variable $X$ \\
  $\mathcal{N}$ & The normal distribution \\ 
  $\mathcal{P}$ & The Poisson distribution \\
  $\bm{x}\sim\mathcal{N}$ & The elements of $\bm{x}$ follows a normal distribution \\
  $\bm{x}_{\mathcal{N}}$ & The same as $\bm{x}\sim\mathcal{N}$ \\
  $\Pr(\bm{x}=a)$ & Probability of the ocurrency of $a$ in that a $\bm{x}$  \\
  $\Pr(\bm{x}=a, \bm{y}=b)$ & $\Pr(\bm{x}=a)$ and $\Pr(\bm{y}=b)$ (joint probability)  \\

  $\Pr(A|B)$ & Conditional probability of $A$ given $B$ \\
  $\mathbb{E}[\bm{x}]$ & Expectation of $\bm{x}$ \\
  $\text{Var}(\bm{x})$ & Variance of $\bm{x}$ \\
  $||\bm{x}||_2$ & $L_2$ norm of $\bm{x}$ \\
  $f_s$ & Sampling frequency \\
  $\mathcal{F}$ & The (forward) Fourier transform ($\mathcal{F}(\bm{x})=\mathbf{X}$) (see Section~\ref{sec:Fourier_transform})\\
  $\mathbf{X}$ & The discrete Fourier transform of the signal $\bm{x}$ \\
  $\mathcal{F}^{-1}$ & The inverse Fourier transform ($\mathcal{F}^{-1}(\mathbf{X})=\bm{x}$)  (see Section~\ref{sec:Fourier_transform})\\
  $\cdot^*$ & the complex conjugate of $\cdot$ \\
  $\bm{x}*\bm{y}$ & $=\mathcal{F}^{-1}(\mathcal{F}(\bm{x})\mathcal{F}(\bm{y}))=\mathcal{F}^{-1}(\mathbf{X}\mathbf{Y})$ (convolution) \\

  $A.b$ & The $b$ component of the data structure $A$ \\
  $(A)b$ & First $A$, then $b$ \\
  $E(\bm{x})$ & Energy of $\bm{x}$ (see Section~\ref{sec:energy_signal}) \\
  $P(\bm{x})$ & Power of $\bm{x}$ (see Section~\ref{sec:power_signal}) \\
  $\text{PS}(\bm{x})$ & Power spectrum of $\bm{x}$ (see Section~\ref{sec:power_spectrum}) \\
  $\text{PSD}(\bm{x})$ & Power spectral density of $\bm{x}$ (see Section~\ref{sec:PSD}) \\
  $\text{CC}(\bm{x},\bm{y})$ & Cross-correlation between $\bm{x}$ and $\bm{y}$ (see Section~\ref{sec:cross-correlation}) \\
  $\text{NCC}(\bm{x},\bm{y})$ & Normalized cross-correlation between $\bm{x}$ and $\bm{y}$ (see Section~\ref{sec:cross-correlation}) \\

  $\bm{I}$ & The identity matrix
